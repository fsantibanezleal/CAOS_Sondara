# data/, what is committed and what is not

Sondara commits its source declarations, a small bundled source subset, the authored validation fixtures and the
scenario registry. The field downloads and every derived output stay out of git: the pipeline reads the downloads
from `$SONDARA_RAW` (or `--cache`) and writes to `build/derived/` (or `--out`).

## Layout

| Path | What | Git |
|---|---|---|
| `sources/manifest.json` | `drillhole.sources/v1`: every source file by URL or bundled path, byte count and SHA-256, and each family's license and attribution | committed |
| `sources/ntgs-12le002/` | the bundled normalized NTGS subset (collars, eleven survey stations, assays of hole 12LE002) and its column mapping | committed |
| `fixtures/F*/`, `fixtures/registry.json` | the 32 authored fixture folders, and the registry of all 42 fixtures, each with its construction, stage, expected result and the test that verifies it; generated by `scripts/fixtures/author_fixtures.py` | committed |
| `interpretations/alberta-lithology-v1.json` | the reviewed lithology mapping of the Alberta logs (five categories after the stratigraphy of MAR_19860002), its rules and evidence, the unmapped codes with their reasons, and the categorical grid; an authored interpretation, MIT | committed |
| `interpretations/rocklea-spectral-products-v1.json` | the reviewed reading of the Rocklea hyperspectral export (R12): each spectral column's product and unit after the collection's product descriptions workbook, the embedded assay columns with their comparison rule, the registration rule, and the stated accuracies of the iron-oxide index with their sources; an authored interpretation, MIT | committed |
| `scenarios/registry.json` | the 32 registered scenarios (R01 to R12, A01 to A08, S01 to S12) with their questions; the scenario matrix resolves them | committed |
| `artifacts/` | the compact exported artifacts the web will read (unit SD-8) | empty until SD-8 |
| `raw/` | never used for downloads; kept ignored so a stray download cannot be committed | ignored |

## The contracts

Files enter the pipeline through the sources manifest (the three field families) or an import manifest
(`drillhole.import/v1`, your own files), and every stage writes a versioned, hashed output. Both boundaries, their
schemas and their checks are in [the data contracts](../docs/architecture/08_data-contracts.md), and the import
manifest is explained step by step in [bring your own data](../docs/guides/02_bring-your-own-data.md).
